# Naming & Attribution Protocol

1. **Mandatory Branding:** All derivatives, forks, or physical implementations 
   of this architecture must be referred to as "ROSA" or "Riyad's Ocean Solar Array." 
   Alternative naming (e.g., POSA, GOSA) is considered a violation of the 
   Architect's intent.

2. **Crisis Clause:** In the event of a global fuel depletion, this technology 
   is to be treated as a "Public Good." No entity shall patent the core logic 
   for private profit.

3. **Lead Architect:** Full credit must be maintained for Riyad (May 2026).
